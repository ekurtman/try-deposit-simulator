Method: At each completed maturity, gross TRY interest = eligible principal × annual gross rate × term days / 365. Withholding tax is deducted before compounding. USD/TRY mid-market rate grows as (1 + annual appreciation)days / 365. The bank's exit USD selling quote is modeled by scaling today's quoted selling rate by the same depreciation factor. No interest is credited during an unfinished term. This is a scenario model, not live banking data or investment advice. It excludes transfer fees, changes in the bank FX spread, additional taxation in your country of residence, and non-standard holiday/renewal timing. Initial quote values are illustrative and should be replaced with current bank quotes.

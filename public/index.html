UNDER THE HOOD

Understand the assumptions.

An educational calculator for exploring possibilities. Scenarios are hypothetical, not forecasts or investment advice.

How interest and FX are calculated

Gross interest = eligible TRY principal × annual gross rate × actual term days / 365. Withholding is deducted from interest, and only net interest is reinvested at maturity. The denominator stays 365, including in leap years.

Exit USD selling quote = initial selling quote × (1 + annual USD/TRY increase / 100)elapsed days / 365. Final USD = final TRY / exit selling quote. The buy/sell quotes capture entry and exit spreads; no extra spread is deducted.

For starting TRY, no entry conversion occurs. The USD benchmark is its immediate conversion value at the initial bank selling quote. Break-even is the annual FX increase that leaves final USD equal to the starting USD benchmark. It is undefined for zero capital or a zero-day horizon.

What happens at renewal or early withdrawal?

The rate is locked when each term begins. The optional rate transition interpolates from the initial rate toward the year-end rate over the first 365 elapsed days, then holds there. Promotional terms override that schedule; when they end, the schedule resumes at the actual renewal day.

An unfinished term earns zero interest in this model. Previously paid interest is retained. The active deposit is broken at exit, with no additional penalty. Actual bank rules may differ. The promotional excluded amount remains accessible cash within the total balance and becomes interest-eligible when the promotion ends.

Model limits, tax, and privacy

Official public bank pages are fetched on demand and cached for up to five minutes. Fetch time is not the bank’s quote-update time; a bank may publish an older quote. Manual defaults remain illustrative. Confirm the published quotes, eligibility, and the editable 17.5% withholding assumption for your account. Tax can vary by account type, opening or renewal date, and maturity.

The model excludes transfer fees, additional personal taxes, changing proportional FX spreads, tiered balances, bank-specific rounding, holidays, and renewal delays. It assumes immediate reinvestment, fixed term length, and a constant withholding rate. It does not model inflation, default risk, or alternative USD interest earnings.

Bank sources are fetched through our server; your capital and scenario inputs are not sent to banks. Inputs are calculated in your browser. Sharing places all assumptions in the URL, so anyone with the link can read them; URLs may also appear in browser history and hosting logs. No analytics or external fonts are used.
